    Scope

    What the app does

    Learn

    • Six-stage path from slip knot to sweater
    • Short lessons with frame-by-frame technique pictures
    • English or continental style, with a left-handed mirror
    • Glossary of abbreviations (K, P, k2tog, ssk, yo)

    Build

    • Pick an item, get a kit list and numbered steps
    • The picture grows as you count rows
    • Patterns resize to your yarn, needles and measurements
    • Row counter, saved progress, works offline

    Practice

    • Name the swatch, read the chart, spot the dropped stitch
    • Abbreviation flashcards
    • Gauge swatch trainer
    • Streaks and stage badges

    Photo coach

    • Check my work: mistakes, tension, where you are
    • Knit from a photo: reference image to guided steps
    • Always asks follow-up questions before giving numbers
    Curriculum

    Photo features

    How the two photo features work

    Check my work

    • You photograph the piece flat, in daylight
    • The model gets the photo plus the step you are on
    • It returns marked problems and a fix for each
    • Reliable for dropped stitches, accidental purls, extra stitches at edges, twisted joins
    • Unreliable for exact stitch counts on large pieces

    Knit from a photo

    • The model names the item, stitch pattern, construction and colour count
    • The app asks for your yarn weight, a gauge swatch and body measurements
    • Those numbers fill a tested template (beanie, scarf, mitts, socks)
    • Works for plain, ribbed and striped items. Complex cables and lace get the closest match, clearly labelled

    Why templates

    • A model guessing stitch counts from a photo produces hats that do not fit
    • Templates carry the arithmetic. The photo only chooses which template and which options
    • Each new template extends what photos can turn into
    Stack

    Recommended technology

    One web codebase that installs on a phone like an app and works offline, so it can sit next to your knitting. It can be wrapped for the app stores later without a rewrite.

    LayerChoiceWhy
    AppReact, TypeScript, Vite, installable PWARuns on any phone, tablet or laptop. Capacitor wraps it for iOS and Android when wanted.
    IllustrationsSVG drawn from pattern dataThe step pictures in this prototype are generated from stitch data, so every pattern gets pictures for free.
    Technique framesHand-drawn SVG sequences, Lottie for motionHands and needles need an illustrator's touch. Small files, crisp at any size.
    PatternsJSON templates with Zod validationTemplates take gauge and measurements and output steps. Lessons live as MDX files in the repo.
    ProgressZustand, IndexedDB via Dexie, WorkboxRow counts and progress save on the device and survive no signal.
    Accounts and photosSupabase (auth, Postgres, storage)Only needed once you want sync between devices or a photo history.
    Photo coachClaude vision API behind a serverless functionKeeps the API key off the phone. Returns structured results that map to template options.
    QualityVitest, Playwright, Vercel hostingPattern arithmetic gets unit tests. A wrong stitch count wastes hours of knitting.
    Plan

    Build order

    Durations are rough estimates for steady part-time work.

    Phase 0 · week 1

    Foundation

    Project scaffold, design tokens, stitch renderer, pattern and lesson schemas.

    Phase 1 · weeks 2–3

    First loops

    Stages 1 and 2, garter scarf builder, row counter, offline progress. Usable with your new yarn.

    Phase 2 · weeks 4–6

    Projects and games

    Beanie and mitts templates, yarn and gauge setup, three practice games.

    Phase 3 · weeks 7–9

    Photo coach

    Check my work first, then knit from a photo for the templates that exist.

    Phase 4 · ongoing

    Advanced

    Stages 5 and 6: gloves, cables, socks, a sweater. Gift log and sharing.

    Your call

    Decisions that shape the build

    Web app or native app

    • Recommended: installable web app first
    • Native only if app-store presence matters from day one

    Just you, or others too

    • Personal use needs no accounts and no server until the photo coach
    • Sharing with friends adds sign-in and sync

    Knitting style

    • English (yarn in right hand) or continental (yarn in left)
    • The first lessons differ, so pick one to illustrate first
